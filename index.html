多引擎协作，结构化分析。 Multi-engine cooperation, structured analysis.

用最少的规则，建立最多的秩序。 With the fewest rules, the greatest order.

MatchStar 是一种面向结构化文本的多引擎分析型语言。6 个独立引擎协作，把源码、配置、日志、协议都变成可查询的扁平状态。 MatchStar is a multi-engine analysis language for structured text. Six independent engines cooperate to turn source, config, logs and protocols into a queryable flat state.

Principles

每一条都是可执行规则 Every rule is executable

拒绝空泛形容词。所有规则可量化、可校验、可逐步确认。 No vague adjectives. All rules are measurable, verifiable, confirmable.



复制一份 Token，你就拥有了一整套设计语言 Copy one Token — own a complete design language

MatchStar 不要求你记住每一条规则——它只要求你在实现时，逐条确认 MUST 是否满足、MUST NOT 是否违反。 MatchStar doesn't ask you to memorize every rule — it only asks that you confirm every MUST is met and every MUST NOT is not violated.
